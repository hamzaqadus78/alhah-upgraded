Our Heritage & Vision

Our story begins long before the establishment of our company. For generations, our family has been connected to the craft of metalwork. Our ancestors were skilled blacksmiths who mastered the art of shaping metal with precision, patience, and dedication. This tradition of craftsmanship and attention to detail became the foundation of the values we carry today.

In 1977, my father transformed this heritage into a professional surgical instruments business. Starting from the local market, he built the company on the principles of quality, trust, and reliability. Through consistent hard work and commitment to excellence, the business gradually earned the confidence of medical professionals and distributors who relied on the instruments we produced.

Today, we proudly continue his legacy. By combining traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing techniques and global standards, we aim to deliver surgical instruments that meet the evolving needs of healthcare professionals. Our vision is to expand our presence internationally while preserving the values of precision, integrity, and dedication that have guided our journey for generations.
